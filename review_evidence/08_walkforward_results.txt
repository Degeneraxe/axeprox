DATASET & METHODOLOGICAL LIMITATIONS:
1. Single Market Regime: All results are derived from a single ~9-hour overnight collection session (approx. 19:30 to 04:30 UTC), representing a single low-volatility Asian/European overnight trading regime. Out-of-sample test windows are relatively short (ranging from 3 minutes to 72 minutes).
2. Temporal Clustering & Effective Sample Size: Spread events cluster heavily in time during brief periods of cross-venue dislocation. As a consequence, observations are not independent, meaning the effective sample size is smaller than the nominal event count, and the reported bootstrap confidence intervals are likely too narrow.
3. Latency Feature Limitations & Venue Identification: Several exchange feeds do not provide native matching-engine timestamps (Binance depth snapshots and Coinbase L2 channel updates lack granular exchange-send timestamps), requiring transit latencies to be estimated via local arrival intervals. These latency features may therefore act primarily as static venue identifiers rather than dynamic indicators of order-flow congestion.
